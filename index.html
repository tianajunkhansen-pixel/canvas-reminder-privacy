Privacy Policy — Canvas Assessment Reminders

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Overview

Canvas Assessment Reminders is a browser extension designed to help students keep track of upcoming assessment deadlines on the University of Canberra's Canvas learning platform. It provides reminders before assignments are due.

2. Information accessed

To provide its core functionality, the extension may access relevant assignment information displayed on the University of Canberra Canvas website, including:

  • Assignment titles
  • Assignment due dates
  • Assignment submission status
  • Course information needed to identify assessments

The extension uses this information to identify upcoming deadlines and schedule reminders.

3. Data storage and processing

The extension uses browser storage, alarms and notifications to support its reminder functionality. Information is processed within the browser to the extent required to identify assignments and manage reminders. Any information stored by the extension is used only for its intended functionality.

4. Data sharing

The extension developer does not sell users' personal information.

5. Permissions

  • Storage: Supports saving extension settings and reminder-related information.
  • Notifications: Displays assignment deadline reminders.
  • Alarms: Schedules reminders for the appropriate times.

The extension runs on the University of Canberra Canvas website to access the assignment information required for its purpose.

6. Third-party services

The extension relies on the University of Canberra Canvas platform to display course and assignment information. If additional third-party services are used, such as an SMS provider, this policy must explain what information is shared with those services and why.

7. Data security and retention

The extension is intended to use assignment information only for its reminder functionality. The handling and retention of any information transmitted to external services must be described here if applicable.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the published privacy policy URL.
